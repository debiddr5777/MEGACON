IS-2185 (Part 3) Compliance

Our manufacturing line cures the block slurry under high-pressure autoclave chambers (at 12 bar pressure and 190°C), triggering the formation of Tobermorite (calcium silicate hydrate mineral).

This mineral crystalline structure gives our blocks high compressive strength while maintaining an incredibly low structural density, ideal for high-rise buildings and seismic zones.
