Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (AAC) is a lightweight, load-bearing, high-insulating, durable building product, produced in a wide range of sizes and strengths.

MEGACON AAC Blocks are manufactured using 100% pure fly ash, OPC cement, quick lime, gypsum, and aluminium powder. Cured under high-pressure steam in autoclaves, they offer double the insulation and up to 3x lighter weight compared to traditional clay bricks.
